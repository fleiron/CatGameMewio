Privacy Policy

App: Games for cats - Mewio
Effective date: December 18, 2025
Contact: ilsuhazs@icloud.com

Quick summary: We don’t ask you to create an account. The app does not use push notifications. The only third-party service used is RevenueCat to manage subscriptions and purchases.

Information We Collect

We collect only what is needed to provide subscriptions and maintain app functionality.

  • Purchases and subscription status (e.g., whether you have an active entitlement, what package was purchased, trial/renewal status).
  • Country/region (used by the subscription provider to help determine offering availability and pricing context).
  • Anonymous identifiers used by the RevenueCat SDK to associate purchases with your device/app user (not an advertising profile).

Apple (App Store) also processes purchases. We do not receive your payment card details.

How We Use Information

  • To provide and verify access to premium features (unlock all games, sounds & custom cat play).
  • To restore purchases and keep your subscription status synced across app launches.
  • To prevent fraud and maintain service reliability.

Third-Party Services

We use RevenueCat to manage in-app subscriptions and purchase verification.

  • RevenueCat may process purchase data and technical identifiers required to operate subscriptions.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

Data Sharing

We share data only with service providers necessary to operate subscriptions (RevenueCat) and with Apple for purchases. We do not share data for advertising.

Data Retention

We retain subscription-related information only as long as necessary to provide premium access and fulfill legal or accounting obligations. RevenueCat and Apple may retain data according to their policies.

Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

Children’s Privacy

This app is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

Your Choices

  • You can manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple ID settings in the App Store.
  • You can request purchase restoration inside the app (Restore Purchases).

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The effective date will be updated at the top of this page.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at ilsuhazs@icloud.com.
